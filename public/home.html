The Problem

A single export order passes through 15–20 small vendors — fabric, dyeing, cutting, stitching, embroidery, packaging. One delayed stage can cause the entire shipment to miss its deadline, triggering steep export penalties. These vendors have no digital footprint, so companies only find out once the delay has already happened.

Why Existing Platforms Fall Short

Large traceability platforms (TrusTrace, TextileGenesis) collect certificates and structured documents — from big, digital-ready suppliers. None of them track the daily operational status of a small, informal, WhatsApp-only vendor. That's exactly the gap EverSupply fills.

Three Pillars

01

SENSE

Capture a real-time signal from every vendor — large or small, digital or WhatsApp-only — in their own language.

02

PREDICT

Turn risk into impact instantly — expected delay, affected stage, and updated resilience score.

03

ACT

Evaluate alternative vendors and recommend the best action — always with human approval before execution.
